0% commission to the seller

Sold DirectThe campaigns
Five campaigns, in sequence

Earn the first twenty, prove the loop, then buy reach.

Campaign 1 · Weeks 4–10

The Founding Twenty

Twenty founding listings across the four launch pockets, white-glove, live on Property24 within 48 hours of mandate. Each one is a case study: a DIY ad became an agency-grade listing and a run sale, at 0%.

Channel: founders' networks, suburb groups, boards
Campaign 2 · Always on

What is my home worth?

The Quick Estimate as the ad. Targeted social to homeowners in the launch suburbs, QR codes on boards and flyers, suburb WhatsApp and community groups. A price range in minutes; a Property Report for the serious.

Channel: Meta geo + interest, community groups, print
Campaign 3 · From week 10

Sold direct, the story

PR on a genuinely useful consumer question: a portal ad is not a sale, so what does a R6m private sale actually take? Then the first sold-direct stories, with consent, street by street.

Channel: property press, radio interviews, our own pages
Campaign 4 · Always on

Neighbours next

Every live listing advertises to the street. A referral reward for the seller who brings the next mandate, and the after-sale community thread that keeps the number in the household's pocket.

Channel: boards, the thread itself, referral links
Campaign 5 · Month 5 onward

Brand, above the line

Outdoor on the Atlantic Seaboard and Southern Suburbs commutes, Cape Town radio, digital video. Unlocked only once the founding cohort's conversion data confirms the model's marketing-to-deals assumption.

Channel: OOH, radio, video; bank sponsor co-funded
Partner co-marketing

The bank headline sponsor

One bank as the featured brand on the platform (advertising, never steering), co-funding above-the-line reach from Year 1. Property24 and BetterBond carry the agency-grade and bond stories in their own channels.

Modelled sponsorship: R3m (Y1) to R8m (Y5)

The first five months

Reach first, then earn the first twenty.

  1. Launch burst · weeks 1–4Four weeks of paid reach on Property24, where every Cape Town seller already looks, paired with Ask Ash, the property media house with over 100,000 followers and columns in the national press. Every Cape Town homeowner sees the brand eight times before the Founding Twenty opens. The portal agreement, practitioner registration and partner letters are concluded alongside.
  2. Founding Twenty · weeks 4–10Twenty founding listings in the four pockets. White-glove concierge; the 48-hour promise; every listing a case study in the making.
  3. Prove the loop · weeks 10–20First registered sales. Measure listing-to-enquiry-to-offer-to-registration, leads per listing, sell-through, acquisition cost by channel, and why sellers chose us.
  4. Scale · month 5 onwardAbove-the-line brand spend only after the conversion data holds. Broaden suburbs; take the wedge story national.

Sold DirectRisks
What could go wrong, and what we do about it

Discipline on acquisition cost is the whole plan.

Marketing-led failure

The Purplebricks guardrail

The comparable that was destroyed by acquisition cost and over-expansion. We land at prime where demand is referral-driven, earn trust before buying reach, and gate every above-the-line rand behind conversion data.

Distribution

Property24 slow-walks the agreement

Sign Private Property first (both portals are bound by the same interoperability rights); our own listing pages and shareable WhatsApp links carry the interim. The relationship is framed as complementary: we are a paying wholesale customer.

Competition

Copycats and portal private tiers

A copycat needs practitioner registration, origination economics to fund 0%, a portal agreement and consumer trust. We move first, own the prime brand, and keep the product depth they would have to rebuild.
