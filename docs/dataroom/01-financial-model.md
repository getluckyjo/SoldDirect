# Sold Direct — 5-Year Financial Model (Prime-led, Tapering; in-house origination from Y2)

All figures **ZAR**. Y1 = first full year of Cape Town operations. Illustrative; rests on the
sourced inputs in the Assumption base. **Strategy:** *land at prime* (R6.5m, biggest rand
savings + bond) and **taper the average price down as the brand scales** — broadening into the
upper-market — to grow from a **much larger addressable pool** and cut the implied market-share
risk. Run by a small **technology-augmented** team with **above-the-line (brand) marketing** driving the
deal ramp. **From Year 2 we bring bond origination in-house, working directly with the banks** —
capturing the full origination commission instead of a thin referral share.

## 1. The monetisation model (legally clean)

1. **Bond origination (free 0% path).**
   - **Year 1 — BetterBond referral.** Qualifying ≥80% bonds are placed via our multi-bank
     originator (BetterBond); buyer keeps rate choice; we earn the **origination referral
     (~0.5% of the bond)**.
   - **Year 2 onward — in-house, direct with banks.** We become our own originator with bank
     aggregation agreements and capture the **full origination commission (~1.5% of the bond)** —
     roughly **3× the referral**. Year 1 via BetterBond buys time to sign the bank agreements and
     put the FAIS/FSP accreditation in place before switching.
2. **1% facilitation fee** on cash / sub-80% / non-partner deals (~6× less than a typical
   full-service commission, 5–7% + VAT).
3. **Bank headline sponsorship** — annual fee (R3m → R8m).
4. **Conveyancer panel advertising** — fixed annual subscriptions (R0.36m → R2.4m),
   volume-independent. **There is no conveyancing referral fee: the Legal Practice Council
   prohibits attorneys paying referral fees.** The seller appoints a panel conveyancer
   (a 30–40% fee discount is passed to the consumer); the panel pays for advertising, never
   per-deal referrals.
5. **Optional ecosystem revenue** (the database; default off — see Ancillary Revenue tab).

## 2. Price taper, the origination step & the revenue engine

The **average transacting price tapers from R6.5m (Y1) to R4.0m (Y5)** — we start prime and broaden
down-market. Layered on top, the **origination rate steps up from 0.5% (Y1, BetterBond) to 1.5%
(Y2+, in-house)**, which lifts revenue per deal from Year 2 even as the price tapers.

### The launch team (Y1–Y2)

Four people run the business to breakeven, so the R5m seed covers the cash trough in every
scenario, the conservative case included, with headroom (§4 and Capital and valuation, §1).

**The Y1 team (4 people, R150k/month, R1.80m/yr + ~R0.05m statutory):**

| Role | Monthly | Annual | Carries |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Managing Director (founder) | R50k | R600k | Management, dealmaking, partnerships, bookkeeping, tech support |
| Marketing Director (founder) | R50k | R600k | All marketing and the seller funnel, dealmaking, tech support |
| Principal practitioner & compliance (PPRA / FFC) | R30k | R360k | Mandates, offers, transfer oversight; the firm's Fidelity Fund Certificate |
| Agent / admin | R20k | R240k | Viewings, document chasing, concierge |
| **Total** | **R150k** | **R1.80m** | |

No agency, no finance hire, no in-house developer: Paysoft carries the build under its
free-development allocation, and the company runs remote-first with no office lease. Y2 adds one
practitioner (R30k) and one agent/admin (R20k), released against signed mandates and modelled from
the start of Y2 to be conservative on cost: six people, R2.29m. From Y3 headcount steps up to 11
once the business is self-funding, founders move to market pay, and the ramp runs to 48 by Y5.
Capacity is the thing to watch: 110 registered deals in Y2 on six people is ~18 deals per head
per year, which leans on the platform doing the admin. The phased deployment and mandate-gated
hiring are the release valve if volume outruns the team.
- The **in-house origination cost line** (bond-consultant cost per bonded deal + a bank-aggregation /
  licensing base + a one-off R1.5m Y2 setup) is modelled explicitly and nets against the uplift.
- The **~14% Y5 margin** is deliberately conservative: no conveyancing referral income (LPC),
  **27% tax**, and a realistic headcount (**48 by Y5**).
- **COGS is validated bottom-up** on the COGS Build tab (~7 direct per-deal lines + payment
  processing, blended low/high per line) supporting the ~12% / ~88% gross-margin input. Excludes
  the in-house origination cost (own opex line) and the concierge team (payroll).

## 5. What we are watching

1. **In-house origination execution** — becoming our own originator needs **bank aggregation
   agreements** and **FAIS/FSP accreditation**; the ~1.5% capture and the origination cost inputs
   we will confirm against a bank term sheet. Year 1 via BetterBond is the
   de-risking runway.
2. **ATL→deals conversion** — the central growth assumption; we validate it in the Cape Town pilot.
3. **Upper-market pool size** (~35k) — an estimate we are firming up. The taper makes the share far safer
   (~4%) but the pool count still needs grounding.
4. **Automated roles at 50% of headcount** — we pilot it before relying on it for the cost base.
5. **Marketing-claim consistency** — "0%" holds for the qualifying-bond path; disclose the 1% fee.
