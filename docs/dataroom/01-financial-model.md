# Sold Direct — 5-Year Financial Model (Prime-led, Tapering; in-house origination from Y2)

All figures **ZAR**. Y1 = first full year of Cape Town operations. Illustrative; rests on the
sourced assumptions in `00-assumptions.md`. **Strategy:** *land at prime* (R6.5m, biggest rand
savings + bond) and **taper the average price down as the brand scales** — broadening into the
upper-market — to grow from a **much larger addressable pool** and cut the implied market-share
risk. Run by a small **AI-augmented** team with **above-the-line (brand) marketing** driving the
deal ramp. **From Year 2 we bring bond origination in-house, working directly with the banks** —
capturing the full origination commission instead of a thin referral share.

## 1. The monetisation model (legally clean)

1. **Bond origination (free 0% path).**
   - **Year 1 — BetterBond referral.** Qualifying ≥80% bonds are placed via our multi-bank
     originator (BetterBond); buyer keeps rate choice; we earn the **origination referral
     (~0.5% of the bond)**.
   - **Year 2 onward — in-house, direct with banks.** We become our own originator with bank
     aggregation agreements and capture the **full origination commission (~1.5% of the bond)** —
     roughly **3× the referral**. Year 1 via BetterBond buys time to sign the bank agreements and
     put the FAIS/FSP accreditation in place before switching.
2. **1% facilitation fee** on cash / sub-80% / non-partner deals (~6× less than a typical
   full-service commission, 5–7% + VAT).
3. **Bank headline sponsorship** — annual fee (R3m → R8m).
4. **Conveyancer panel advertising** — fixed annual subscriptions (R0.36m → R2.4m),
   volume-independent. **The old conveyancing referral fee is REMOVED — the Legal Practice
   Council prohibits attorneys paying referral fees.** The seller appoints a panel conveyancer
   (a 30–40% fee discount is passed to the consumer); the panel pays for advertising, never
   per-deal referrals.
5. **Optional ecosystem revenue** (the database; default off — see Ancillary Revenue tab).

## 2. Price taper, the origination step & the revenue engine

The **average transacting price tapers from R6.5m (Y1) to R4.0m (Y5)** — we start prime and broaden
down-market. Layered on top, the **origination rate steps up from 0.5% (Y1, BetterBond) to 1.5%
(Y2+, in-house)**, which lifts revenue per deal from Year 2 even as the price tapers.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Avg transacting price** | R6.5m | R5.75m | R5.0m | R4.5m | R4.0m |
| Bonded share (≥80%) | 40% | 40% | 40% | 42% | 42% |
| **Origination rate on the bond** | **0.5%** *(BetterBond)* | **1.5%** *(in-house)* | **1.5%** | **1.5%** | **1.5%** |
| Origination income / qualifying deal | R29,250 | R77,625 | R67,500 | R60,750 | R54,000 |
| Conveyancing referral | **R0 — removed (LPC prohibition)** | R0 | R0 | R0 | R0 |
| **Revenue per deal** | **R55,700** | **R70,550** | **R62,500** | **R57,615** | **R52,380** |
| Registered deals | 50 | 110 | 325 | 750 | 1,450 |
| Bank sponsorship (Rm/yr) | 3.0 | 4.0 | 5.0 | 6.5 | 8.0 |

### The bootstrap operating stance (Y1–Y2)

The pre-breakeven period runs on a **four-person team** so the R5m seed covers the cash trough
in every scenario, the conservative case included, with headroom (§4 and
`02-capital-and-valuation.md` §1). Revenue, marketing and the regulatory/compliance line are
**untouched** — the stance changes how we operate, not what drives the ramp.

**The Y1 team (4 people, R150k/month, R1.80m/yr + ~R0.05m statutory):**

| Role | Monthly | Annual | Carries |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Managing Director (founder) | R50k | R600k | Management, dealmaking, partnerships, bookkeeping, tech support |
| Marketing Director (founder) | R50k | R600k | All marketing and the seller funnel, dealmaking, tech support |
| Principal practitioner & compliance (PPRA / FFC) | R30k | R360k | Mandates, offers, transfer oversight; the firm's Fidelity Fund Certificate |
| Agent / admin | R20k | R240k | Viewings, document chasing, concierge |
| **Total** | **R150k** | **R1.80m** | |

The founders are paid **R50k/month each — below market for both roles — and do all marketing,
management, dealmaking, bookkeeping and tech support themselves**; no agency, no finance hire, no
in-house developer (Paysoft carries the build under its free-development allocation). Versus the
CA-reviewed cost base (R7.80m Y1 / R19.93m Y2) the stance saves **~R1.95m in Y1 and ~R4.05m in
Y2 — ~R6.0m before tax**:

| Measure | Y1 | Y2 | Saving |
|---|---|---|---:|
| **Four-person team**, founders at R50k/month carrying the functions above; Y2 hires (one practitioner at R30k, one agent/admin at R20k) released only against signed mandates and modelled from the start of Y2 to be conservative on cost | **4** heads (was 8) | **6** heads (was 14) | ~R1.55m / ~R3.80m |
| **No office lease**: remote-first, co-working day passes, viewings happen at the property | — | — | ~R0.25m / ~R0.15m |
| **Paysoft free-development allocation + startup credits** carry the Y1 build and most Y2 tooling (cloud, BSP, SaaS) | — | — | ~R0.15m / ~R0.10m |
| **Total** | | | **~R1.95m / ~R4.05m** |

From Y3 headcount steps up to 11 (a deliberate
catch-up hire once the business is self-funding), founders move to market pay, and the
delivery-realistic ramp to 48 by Y5 stands. ⚠ Capacity is the thing to watch: 110 registered deals in Y2 on six people is ~18
deals per head per year, which leans on the platform doing the admin. The milestone tranches
and mandate-gated hiring are the release valve if volume outruns the team.
- The **in-house origination cost line** (bond-consultant cost per bonded deal + a bank-aggregation /
  licensing base + a one-off R1.5m Y2 setup) is modelled explicitly and nets against the uplift.
- The **~14% Y5 margin** reflects the CA review: conveyancing referral
  removed (LPC), **27% tax**, and delivery-realistic headcount (**48 by Y5**) — deliberately
  conservative and far more defensible in diligence than the earlier ~39%.
- **COGS is validated bottom-up** on the COGS Build tab (~7 direct per-deal lines + payment
  processing, blended low/high per line) supporting the ~12% / ~88% gross-margin input. Excludes
  the in-house origination cost (own opex line) and the concierge team (payroll).

## 5. Watch-outs (flag before investors do)

1. **In-house origination execution** — becoming our own originator needs **bank aggregation
   agreements** and **FAIS/FSP accreditation**; the ~1.5% capture and the origination cost inputs
   are assumptions to confirm against a real bank term sheet. Year 1 via BetterBond is the
   de-risking runway.
2. **ATL→deals conversion** — the central growth assumption; validate with a CT pilot.
3. **Upper-market pool size** (~35k) — an estimate; confirm. The taper makes the share far safer
   (~4%) but the pool count still needs grounding.
4. **AI agents at 50% of roles** — pilot before relying on it for the cost base.
5. **Marketing-claim consistency** — "0%" holds for the qualifying-bond path; disclose the 1% fee.
